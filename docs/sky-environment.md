# Painted sky environment

The orbiting 3D viewer uses [sky-panorama-v2.png](../assets/sky-panorama-v2.png), a 1774 × 887 painted environment texture, on a distant sky dome. Broad unequal cloud banks and open blue sky leave more room for the island silhouettes throughout the orbit. The islands, buildings, vegetation, water, and travelers remain real geometry. The five styles grade this same sky along with the shared scene materials.

The current image was generated with the built-in image generation tool in **generate mode**. The previous [v1 panorama](../assets/sky-panorama-v1.png) remains in the repository; it was made in edit mode using [painted-sky.png](../assets/painted-sky.png) as its reference. No external texture service is required at runtime.

## Current generation prompt

> Use case: stylized-concept. Create a production sky environment texture for a painterly floating-island fantasy world. A seamless horizontally repeating 360 degree panorama, 2:1 landscape aspect ratio, highest practical resolution. Sky and clouds only, no land, architecture, objects, birds, text, border or watermark. Hand-painted gouache animation-background craft: large sculptural cumulonimbus clouds with warm ivory sunlight, nuanced pale blue/lavender shadow faces, soft watercolor edges combined with confident broad brush planes. Rich natural cerulean and turquoise atmosphere, NOT electric ultramarine, no photographic realism, no tiny speckled texture. Composition is extremely important: the upper 60 percent is mostly calm open blue sky with very sparse delicate wisps; the lower 40 percent has a few broad unequal cloud banks and a soft distant cloud sea. Two taller but softly contrasted cumulus groups can rise at quarter and three-quarter width, leaving generous blue openings through the center and near the left/right seam. Leave room for a 3D island silhouette in the middle of the texture. Clouds have convincing voluminous form with large readable light and shadow masses; avoid a wallpaper of thousands of equally small cloud lumps. One consistent warm sun direction, no sun disk or lens flares. Match left and right edge colors and shapes seamlessly. This texture supports all the orbit angles of an interactive 3D landscape, so no dramatic centered vanishing point, no fake ground horizon, and no vignette. It should feel serene, luminous, expansive, richly painted, and compositionally quiet.

## Previous v1 generation prompt

> Edit and extend this painted sky into a seamless 360-degree equirectangular environment panorama, aspect ratio 2:1, at the highest practical resolution. This will be the sky surrounding a real 3D floating-island scene. Preserve the reference's rich painterly fantasy animation background quality: saturated cobalt and cyan blue sky, magnificent structured cumulonimbus banks, many intricate nested billows, warm ivory sunlit edges and subtly cool blue-violet undersides, delicate brushwork and gouache washes, luminous atmosphere. Arrange impressive but varied cloud banks across the full width, with generous blue openings, high delicate wisps, large billowing clouds near the horizon and a soft cloud sea below. Match the left and right edges seamlessly in both color and cloud shapes, for horizontal repeat. Avoid obvious forms pinching at the top and bottom poles. Lighting should have one broad consistent source, without a visible sun disk or lens flare. Preserve nuanced handmade painting, never plastic smooth 3D blobs. Sky and clouds only: no terrain, islands, buildings, people, birds, lettering, watermark, frame or border. Composition must work as a panoramic texture, rather than a framed illustration.

The [atmosphere module](../assets/sky-castle-atmosphere.js) handles directional mapping, palette grading, filtered texture sampling, and slight periodic drift. Its wider angular framing brings a cloud bank to one side of the main island and a cloud sea below it, with open blue behind the summit. The Fantasy grade gives the blue sky and shaded cloud faces more color separation while keeping cream highlights. Soft polar haze blends away the painting's edge rows at steep viewing angles. Dolly zoom and camera translation do not magnify or move the distant sky.

The viewer waits for the texture before exposing its frame-capture API. The sky is a painted backdrop, not a volumetric weather simulation.
